# CD4053B — Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD4053B is a CMOS triple 2-channel analog multiplexer/demultiplexer with logic-level conversion. It contains three separate single-pole, double-throw analog switches (A, B, C), each independently controlled by a digital select line. The device has digitally-controlled analog switches with low ON impedance and very low OFF leakage current. It dissipates extremely low quiescent power over the full VDD-VSS and VDD-VEE supply-voltage ranges, independent of the logic state of the control signals.

## Features

- Wide range of digital and analog signal levels: Digital 3V to 20V, Analog up to 20V peak-to-peak
- Single supply range: 3V to 20V
- Dual supply range: +/-3V to +/-10V
- Low ON resistance: 125 Ohm typical over full input range at VDD = 15V
- Low channel leakage: +/-10 pA typical at VDD = 15V
- Low quiescent power dissipation: 0.2 uW typical
- Break-before-make switching eliminates channel overlap
- Bidirectional signal path
- ESD protection: HBM 3000V, CDM 2000V
