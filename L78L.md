# L78L — STMicroelectronics

## Description

The L78L series are three-terminal positive voltage regulators employing internal current limiting and thermal shutdown, making them essentially indestructible. If adequate heat-sinking is provided, they can deliver up to 100 mA output current. They are intended as fixed voltage regulators in a wide range of applications including local or on-card regulation for elimination of noise and distribution problems associated with single-point regulation. They can also be used with power pass elements to make high-current voltage regulators, or as Zener diode/resistor combination replacements with lower quiescent current and lower noise.

## Features

- Output current up to 100 mA
- Output voltages of 3.3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 18, 24 V
- Thermal overload protection
- Short-circuit protection
- No external components required
- Available in +/-4% (A) or +/-8% (C) selection

## Typical Application Circuits

The L78L can be used as a basic fixed-voltage regulator with a 0.33 uF input capacitor and 0.1 uF output capacitor. Additional application circuits include high output current with external pass transistor (short-circuit protected), output boost circuit, current regulator, and adjustable output regulator with external resistor divider.
