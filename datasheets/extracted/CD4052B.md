# CD4052B — Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD4052B is a CMOS dual 4-channel analog multiplexer/demultiplexer with logic-level conversion. It is digitally controlled with low ON impedance and very low OFF leakage current. The device dissipates extremely low quiescent power over the full VDD-VSS and VDD-VEE supply-voltage ranges, independent of the logic state of the control signals. It features break-before-make switching to eliminate channel overlap and supports bidirectional signal paths.

## Features

- Wide range of digital and analog signal levels: Digital 3V to 20V, Analog up to 20Vp-p
- Single supply range: 3V to 20V
- Dual supply range: +/-3V to +/-10V
- Low ON resistance: 125 Ohm typical over range for VDD = 15V
- Low channel leakage: +/-10 pA typical at VDD = 15V
- Low quiescent power dissipation: 0.2 uW typical
- Break-before-make switching eliminates channel overlap
- Bidirectional signal path
- ESD protection HBM: 3000V, CDM: 2000V
- Pin compatible with industry standard 4052 muxes

## Typical Application Circuits

The CD4052B can be used with a microcontroller to multiplex multiple analog signals through a pair of common channels (X and Y), controlled by the B and A select inputs. Typical applications include analog signal multiplexing/demultiplexing, A/D and D/A conversion, and signal gating. A 0.1 uF bypass capacitor on VCC is recommended; for dual supply, 0.1 uF on each supply pin.
