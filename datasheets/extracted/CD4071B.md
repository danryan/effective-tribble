# CD4071B -- Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD4071B, CD4072B, and CD4075B OR gates provide the system designer with direct implementation of the positive-logic OR function and supplement the existing family of CMOS gates. CD4071B is a quad 2-input OR gate, CD4072B is a dual 4-input OR gate, and CD4075B is a triple 3-input OR gate. The devices are supplied in 14-lead packages and are high-voltage types (20-Volt Rating).

## Features

- Medium-speed operation: tPHL, tPLH = 60 ns (typ.) at VDD = 10 V
- 100% tested for quiescent current at 20 V
- Maximum input current of 1 uA at 18 V over full package-temperature range; 100 nA at 18 V and 25 C
- Standardized, symmetrical output characteristics
- Noise margin (over full package temperature range): 1 V at VDD = 5 V, 2 V at VDD = 10 V, 2.5 V at VDD = 15 V
- 5-V, 10-V, and 15-V parametric ratings
- Meets all requirements of JEDEC Tentative Standard No. 13B
