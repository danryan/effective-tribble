# CD40174B — Harris Semiconductor / Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD40174B is a CMOS hex D-type flip-flop with a common clock and common asynchronous clear. It contains six identical D-type flip-flops that transfer data from the D inputs to the Q outputs on the positive-going transition of the clock input. The clear input asynchronously resets all outputs to logic low when driven low.

## Features

- Six D-type flip-flops with common clock
- Common asynchronous clear (active low)
- Wide supply voltage range: 3 V to 18 V
- Buffered Q outputs
- Positive-edge-triggered clock
- Low quiescent power dissipation
- 5 V, 10 V, and 15 V parametric ratings
