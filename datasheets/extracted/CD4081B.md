# CD4081B — Harris Semiconductor / Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD4081B is a CMOS quad 2-input AND gate. It provides four independent 2-input AND gate functions in a single 14-pin package. The device features high noise immunity, wide supply voltage range (3 V to 18 V), and low power consumption typical of CMOS logic.

## Features

- Quad 2-input AND gate
- Wide supply voltage range: 3 V to 18 V
- High noise immunity: 0.45 VDD (typical)
- Low quiescent power dissipation
- Standardized symmetrical output characteristics
- 5 V, 10 V, and 15 V parametric ratings
- Input current: +/-10 mA max
- Maximum power dissipation: 500 mW
