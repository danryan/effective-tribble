# CD4007UB -- Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD4007UB is a CMOS dual complementary pair plus inverter. It is comprised of three n-channel and three p-channel enhancement-type MOS transistors. The transistor elements are accessible through the package terminals to provide a convenient means for constructing various typical circuits. More complex functions are possible using multiple packages. Numbers shown in parentheses indicate terminals that are connected together to form the various configurations. The device is a high-voltage type (20-Volt Rating).

## Features

- Standardized symmetrical output characteristics
- Medium speed operation: tPHL, tPLH = 30 ns (typ.) at 10 V
- 100% tested for quiescent current at 20 V
- Meets all requirements of JEDEC Tentative Standard No. 13B
- Maximum input current of 1 uA at 18 V over full package-temperature range; 100 nA at 18 V and 25 C
