# 2N3906 -- ON Semiconductor (Fairchild)

## Description

The 2N3906 is a PNP general-purpose amplifier and switching transistor designed for applications at collector currents of 10 mA to 100 mA. Also available as MMBT3906 (SOT-23) and PZT3906 (SOT-223).

## Features

- PNP general-purpose amplifier and switching transistor
- Collector-emitter breakdown voltage: -40 V
- DC current gain (hFE): 100 to 300 at IC = -10 mA
- Current gain-bandwidth product (fT): 250 MHz
- Complementary to 2N3904
