# LM318 — Texas Instruments

## Description

The LM318-N series (LM118-N, LM218-N, LM318-N) are precision high-speed operational amplifiers designed for applications requiring wide bandwidth and high slew rate. They feature a factor of ten increase in speed over general purpose devices without sacrificing DC performance. Internal unity gain frequency compensation simplifies application. External frequency compensation may be added for optimum performance. Feedforward compensation boosts slew rate to over 150 V/us for inverting applications. The LM218-N is specified over -25°C to +85°C and the LM318-N is specified from 0°C to +70°C.

## Features

- 15 MHz small signal bandwidth
- Ensured 50 V/us slew rate
- Maximum bias current of 250 nA
- Operates from supplies of ±5 V to ±20 V
- Internal frequency compensation
- Input and output overload protected
- Pin compatible with general purpose op amps

## Typical Application Circuits

### Fast Voltage Follower

Do not hard-wire as voltage follower; use R1 >= 5 kohm in feedback path. Connect noninverting input to signal, output through R1 to inverting input.

### Fast Summing Amplifier

Use feedforward compensation for improved slew rate (>150 V/us). Add a 10 k / 5 pF network in series with the input for integrator or slow inverter configurations to prevent oscillation.
