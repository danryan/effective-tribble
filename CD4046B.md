# CD4046B — Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD4046B is a CMOS micropower phase-locked loop (PLL) consisting of a low-power, linear voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) and two different phase comparators having a common signal input amplifier and a common comparator input. A 5.2-V zener diode is provided for supply regulation if necessary. The CD4046B types are supplied in 16-lead hermetic dual-in-line ceramic packages (F3A suffix), 16-lead dual-in-line plastic packages (E suffix), 16-lead small-outline packages (NSR suffix), and 16-lead thin shrink small-outline packages (PW and PWR suffixes).

## Features

- Very low power consumption: 70 uW (typ.) at VCO f0 = 10 kHz, VDD = 5V
- Operating frequency range up to 1.4 MHz (typ.) at VDD = 10V, R1 = 5 kOhm
- Low frequency drift: 0.04%/°C (typ.) at VDD = 10V
- Choice of two phase comparators: Exclusive-OR network (I) and edge-controlled memory network with phase-pulse output for lock indication (II)
- High VCO linearity: less than 1% (typ.) at VDD = 10V
- VCO inhibit control for ON-OFF keying and ultra-low standby power consumption
- Source-follower output of VCO control input (demodulated output)
- Zener diode to assist supply regulation
- Standardized, symmetrical output characteristics
- 100% tested for quiescent current at 20V
- 5V, 10V, and 15V parametric ratings

## Typical Application Circuits

The CD4046B is commonly used as a CMOS phase-locked loop. Phase comparator I (exclusive-OR type) operates analogously to an overdriven balanced mixer, and the signal and comparator input frequencies must have a 50% duty cycle to maximize lock range. Phase comparator II (edge-controlled digital memory) acts only on positive edges of the signal and comparator inputs, and its lock range equals the capture range. The VCO requires one external capacitor C1 and one or two external resistors (R1 and optionally R2). R1 and C1 determine the frequency range, while R2 enables a frequency offset. The VCO can be connected through frequency dividers to the comparator input. Typical applications include FM demodulation, frequency synthesis, tone decoding, FSK modems, data synchronization, and signal conditioning.
