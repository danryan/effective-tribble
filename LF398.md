# LF398 — Texas Instruments

## Description

The LF398-N is a monolithic sample-and-hold circuit that uses BI-FET technology to obtain ultrahigh DC accuracy with fast acquisition of signal and low droop rate. Operating as a unity-gain follower, DC gain accuracy is 0.002% typical and acquisition time is as low as 6 us to 0.01%. A bipolar input stage achieves low offset voltage and wide bandwidth. Input impedance of 10^10 Ohm allows high-source impedances without degrading accuracy. P-channel JFETs combined with bipolar devices in the output amplifier give droop rates as low as 5 mV/min with a 1 uF hold capacitor. Logic inputs are fully differential with low input current, allowing direct connection to TTL, PMOS, and CMOS. Differential threshold is 1.4V. The device operates from +/-5V to +/-18V supplies.

## Features

- Operates from +/-5V to +/-18V supplies
- Less than 10 us acquisition time
- Logic input compatible with TTL, PMOS, CMOS
- 0.5 mV typical hold step at Ch = 0.01 uF
- Low input offset
- 0.002% gain accuracy
- Low output noise in hold mode
- Input characteristics do not change during hold mode
- High supply rejection ratio in sample or hold
- Wide bandwidth

## Typical Application Circuits

- X1000 Sample and Hold: Uses LM108 op-amp with LF198-N for amplified sample-and-hold with gain of 1000
- Sample and Difference: Output follows input in hold mode, VOUT = VB + delta VIN
- Ramp Generator with Variable Reset Level
- Integrator with Programmable Reset Level
- Fast Acquisition, Low Droop Sample and Hold (using two LFx98x devices with LM3905 timer)
- Synchronous Correlator for recovering signals below noise level
- 2-Channel Switch using the hold capacitor pin as a second input
- DC and AC Zeroing using offset adjust and inverter with AC-coupled potentiometer
