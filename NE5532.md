# NE5532 — Texas Instruments

## Description

The NE5532, NE5532A, SA5532, and SA5532A devices are high-performance operational amplifiers combining excellent DC and AC characteristics. These devices feature very low noise, high output-drive capability, high unity-gain and maximum-output-swing bandwidths, low distortion, high slew rate, input-protection diodes, and output short-circuit protection. These operational amplifiers are compensated internally for unity-gain operation and have specified maximum limits for equivalent input noise voltage.

## Features

- Equivalent input noise voltage: 5 nV/sqrt(Hz) typical at 1 kHz
- Unity-gain bandwidth: 12 MHz typical
- Common-mode rejection ratio: 100 dB typical
- High DC voltage gain: 100 V/mV typical
- High slew rate: 5 V/us typical

## Typical Application Circuits

**Single-Ended to Differential Output Conversion:** Converts a single-ended input of 2 V to 10 V into a differential output of +/-8 V on a single 15 V supply. Uses two amplifiers: one as buffer creating VOUT+, the second inverts the input and adds a reference voltage to generate VOUT-.

- Supply voltage: 15 V
- Reference voltage: 12 V
- Input: 2 V to 10 V
- Output differential: +/-8 V
