# CD40106B — Texas Instruments

## Description

The CD40106B device consists of six Schmitt-Trigger inputs. Each circuit functions as an inverter with Schmitt-Trigger input. The trigger switches at different points for positive- and negative-going signals. The difference between the positive-going voltage (VP) and the negative-going voltage (VN) is defined as hysteresis voltage (VH). The device is available in CDIP, PDIP, SOIC, SO, and TSSOP packages and is rated for -55°C to +125°C operation.

## Features

- Schmitt-Trigger inputs
- Hysteresis voltage (typical): 0.9 V at VDD = 5 V, 2.3 V at VDD = 10 V, 3.5 V at VDD = 15 V
- Noise immunity greater than 50%
- No limit on input rise and fall times
- Standardized, symmetrical output characteristics
- Maximum input current of 1 uA at 18 V over full package temperature range (100 nA at 18 V and 25°C)
- Low VDD and VSS current during slow input ramp
- 5 V, 10 V, and 15 V parametric ratings

## Typical Application Circuits

### Wave Shaper

Connect input signal through one of the six inverter channels. The Schmitt-Trigger input converts a sine wave to a clean square wave output. Frequency range is DC to 1 MHz.

### Astable Multivibrator

Connect a resistor from output to input, with a capacitor from input to ground. Oscillation frequency: tA = RC * ln[(VP/VN) * ((VDD - VN)/(VDD - VP))]. Recommended ranges: 50 kohm <= R <= 1 Mohm, 100 pF <= C <= 1 uF.

### Monostable Multivibrator

Use with CD4007UB transistor. Timing: tM = RC * ln(VDD / (VDD - VP)). Recommended ranges: 50 kohm <= R <= 1 Mohm, 100 pF <= C <= 1 uF.
