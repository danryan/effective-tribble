# LM339 — Texas Instruments

## Description

The LM339 family (LM139, LM239, LM339, LM339B, LM2901, LM2901B) consists of four independent voltage comparators designed to operate from a single power supply over a wide range of voltages. Quiescent current is independent of the supply voltage. The LM339B and LM2901B are next-generation versions featuring lower offset voltage, higher supply voltage capability, lower supply current, lower input bias current, lower propagation delay, and improved 2 kV ESD performance. The open-collector outputs are compatible with TTL, MOS, and CMOS logic.

## Features

- Four independent voltage comparators
- Wide single-supply voltage range: 2 V to 36 V (B-version), 2 V to 30 V (non-B)
- Low supply current: 0.8 mA typical (B-version), 0.8 mA typical (non-B)
- Common-mode input voltage range includes ground
- Differential input voltage range equal to maximum-rated supply voltage
- Low output saturation voltage
- Output compatible with TTL, MOS, and CMOS
- Open-collector output configuration
- ESD rating (HBM): 2 kV
- Low input offset voltage: 0.37 mV typical (B-version)
- Low input bias current: 3.5 nA typical (B-version)

## Typical Application Circuits

### Single-Ended Comparator

Connect the reference voltage to one input (e.g., IN-) and the signal to the other input (IN+). Add a pull-up resistor from the output to the logic supply voltage (VLOGIC). A bypass capacitor on VCC is recommended.

### Differential Comparator

Connect the two signals to be compared to IN+ and IN-. Add a pull-up resistor from the output to the logic supply voltage. The open-collector output allows level shifting to any logic voltage independent of VCC.
