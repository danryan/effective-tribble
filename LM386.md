# LM386 — Texas Instruments

## Description

The LM386 is a power amplifier designed for use in low voltage consumer applications. The gain is internally set to 20 to keep external part count low, but the addition of an external resistor and capacitor between pins 1 and 8 increases the gain to any value from 20 to 200. The inputs are ground referenced while the output automatically biases to one-half the supply voltage. The quiescent power drain is only 24 mW when operating from a 6 V supply, making the LM386 ideal for battery operation.

## Features

- Battery operation
- Minimum external parts
- Wide supply voltage range: 4 V to 12 V (LM386N-1/-3, LM386M-1) or 5 V to 18 V (LM386N-4)
- Low quiescent current drain: 4 mA
- Voltage gains from 20 to 200
- Ground-referenced input
- Self-centering output quiescent voltage
- Low distortion: 0.2% (AV = 20, VS = 6 V, RL = 8 ohm, PO = 125 mW, f = 1 kHz)

## Typical Application Circuits

### LM386 with Gain = 20 (Minimum Parts)

Connect signal to pin 3 (+INPUT) through a 10 kohm potentiometer. Leave pins 1 and 8 open for gain of 20. Connect a 250 uF coupling capacitor from pin 5 (VOUT) to the speaker. Connect a 0.05 uF capacitor and 10 ohm resistor in series from output to ground for stability. Bypass pin 7 with a 10 uF capacitor. Supply voltage: 5 V to 12 V. Load impedance: 4 ohm to 32 ohm.

### LM386 with Gain = 200

Place a 10 uF capacitor between pins 1 and 8 to bypass the internal 1.35 kohm resistor, increasing gain to 200 (46 dB). A series resistor with the capacitor sets intermediate gain values.
